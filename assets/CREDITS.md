# Third-party assets

Everything below is **CC0 1.0** (public domain) unless noted. No attribution is required;
credit is given as thanks.

## Quaternius (https://quaternius.com)

- `monsters/`: "Ultimate Monsters" pack
- `buildings/`: "Ultimate Fantasy RTS" pack (files renamed to card ids)
- `nature/`: "Stylized Nature MegaKit" (Standard), https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html,
  downloaded from the OpenGameArt mirror https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-nature-megakit (CC0).
  Normal maps dropped and textures downscaled to 512 px.

## Kenney (https://kenney.nl)

- `candy/`: "Food Kit" (lollypop, cupcake, donut-sprinkles, ice-cream, popsicle, candy-bar,
  cookie-chocolate, sundae + `Textures/colormap.png`), from the OpenGameArt mirror
  https://opengameart.org/content/food-kit (CC0).
- `fx/particles/`: "Particle Pack", from the OpenGameArt mirror
  https://opengameart.org/content/particle-pack-80-sprites (CC0). Downscaled to 128 px and
  converted to white with brightness as alpha.
